Control your Athom Homey directly from your wrist with the HomeyWatch app for Wear OS.

This companion app facilitates seamless and secure authorization between your smartwatch and Homey Pro, while allowing you to choose your favorite devices and quick action tiles.

Key Features:
- Seamless pairing: Sign in effortlessly on your phone with automatic token delivery to your watch.
- Quick Actions Tile: Choose up to 9 favorite devices with 4 instant actions on your watch tile.
- Fast and secure: Direct communication without third-party intermediate cloud servers.
