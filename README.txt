Control your Athom Homey directly from your wrist with the HomeyWatch app for Wear OS. This companion app bridges your smartwatch and Homey Pro, enabling effortless smart home control, device status monitoring, and automation triggers without needing your phone for everyday tasks.

Easily configure your favorite devices, customized quick-action tiles, and custom labels directly from the Homey app settings. With fast, secure pairing and direct communication between your watch and Homey Pro, your smart home is always just a glance away.
